Geo Studio – layered 3D geological model from drillhole data

Load collars, surveys, lithology (and assays, topography points) → multi-unit implicit model with topography and a fault → cutaway 3D, editable sections, grade estimation, statistics, export. Runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
Add your data: pick all your CSV files at once; they are sent to the right box by file name (collar, survey, litho, assay, topo, struct, qaqc). Or add one box at a time with its own “Choose file” button, or paste text.

Units

Each unit gets its own implicit interpolant (indicator RBF). Layers: dip 0 and a high anisotropy give smooth sub-horizontal layers; intrusions: anisotropy 1 (isotropic). Dip/dir are the trend of the unit (0 = flat layers).

Faults (planar, vertical slip, any number)

Coordinates are in the same system as your data. Hanging wall = the side the fault dips toward.

Grid & interpolation

Focused shell for a thin unit (vein)

The block model above uses coarse cells, so thin veins disappear. This builds a fine implicit shell for one unit only, around its own samples.
Vein model: uses each drillhole intercept of the chosen unit (hanging-wall and footwall contacts and true thickness). It interpolates a mid-surface and a thickness field, then draws the vein between them, like a Leapfrog vein system. Thin veins keep their real thickness at any cell size.

Practice tool. Models are simplified (planar faults with vertical slip, argmax of per-unit RBF, IDW/ordinary kriging). They are not a substitute for Leapfrog Geo or other validated software, and not a reportable resource.
